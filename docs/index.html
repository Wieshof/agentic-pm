You don't need another productivity app.

When you have too much on your plate, project tools usually create more work: logging into dashboards, updating statuses, copying notes from emails, and remembering which task blocks which.

Meanwhile, using mainstream AI assistants means uploading your client lists, internal emails, and business plans to external servers (and hoping they stay private).

There is a simpler way.

How your project buddy helps you

No new interface to learn. It works quietly in the background on top of the files and channels you already use every day.

01

One calm morning briefing instead of five open tabs

Every morning at 8:00 AM, you get a short email (or spoken summary) in your preferred language telling you what actually needs attention today and what can wait.

02

Spots hidden chain reactions automatically

Your buddy connects the dots across your task list or team tracker so deadlines don't slip silently.

"Heads up: Friday's client launch depends on the printer proof in row 63, which hasn't been confirmed yet."
03

Talk or message normally: let it do the admin

Prefer sending a quick voice note, an email reply, or a chat message? Speak naturally, and your buddy turns messy updates into clean, structured documentation on the right task.

"Fixed the login bug by increasing the cache size."
04

Works for solo operators and small teams alike

  • Working solo? Point it at a plain .xlsx or .csv file on your desktop. Zero setup overhead.
  • Working with a team? Everyone stays in the channel they already use (email, Signal, Matrix, or voice). No new software to learn and no extra user licenses to buy.

Your business stays on your hardware.

Built for people who want practical AI assistance without sacrificing data sovereignty or confidentiality.

100% Local AI

Built to run on local models (like Ollama) directly on your Mac or PC. Turn off your Wi-Fi: it still works.

Zero Cloud Lock-in

No external cloud accounts, no monthly SaaS fees, and zero risk of your data being used to train third-party AI models.

No Separate Database

It never hoards your tasks in a proprietary format. Your Excel file, GitHub, Jira, or OpenProject stays your single source of truth.

Open Source (Apache 2.0)

Transparent, auditable code with no hidden tracking or telemetry.

What do I actually need?

No server farm or enterprise IT department required.

Does my computer support this?

Any modern Mac (M1, M2, M3, or M4) or standard Windows/Linux laptop with 16 GB of memory runs local models smoothly.

How does the local AI work?

It uses Ollama (a free, one-click app for Mac, Windows, and Linux) to run open-weights language models privately on your own hardware.

What does it cost?

Nothing. agentic-pm is free and open source under the Apache 2.0 license, with zero subscriptions, API fees, or per-user charges.
